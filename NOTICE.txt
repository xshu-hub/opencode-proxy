OpenCode Proxy derives from the OpenCode bridge core at
https://github.com/xshu-hub/magpie/tree/210234a5f33e0f2643f82b4337e249cf0fe81371/addons/opencode-bridge
Original bridge version: 0.13.0.
Retains the original MIT license and copyright notice.

Since 0.9.0 this package is a Magpie provider plugin. It does not bundle the
Magpie application, a standalone gateway, or a desktop/frontend application.
Inference runs through the user's real global OpenCode program.

JSON Schema validation uses Ajv, ajv-formats and their runtime dependencies.
Their license texts are included in docs/third-party-licenses.txt.
